# 08 · Voyages, routing, canals, ice

Files: `actions.planVoyage/launch/depart/runPlan/setSpeed/fuelToFinish`, `sim/routing.js`,
`sim/context.js`, `data/distances.js` (generated by `tools/build_distances.mjs`), `data/world.js`
(grid from `tools/build_world.mjs`), `game.sailHour/arrive`, `sim/ice.js`, `sim/orders.js` (standing orders).

## Planning (`planVoyage(state, ctx, ship, to, speed)`)

Speed clamped 6…`maxSpeed`. Errors: not in port; same port; **draft** (`fitsPort`); **pollution ban**
of destination flag; **blacklist** closed port; contract cargo must go to its destination; traded
cargo needs a port that handles the hold; no route; **range** (`needFuel > tank`).

```
seaDays  = nm / (24 × speed × skillSpeed)
needFuel = burnPerDay × equip 'burn' × seaDays × 1.08 + idleBurn
fuelCost = needFuel × fuelPrice(origin)
eta      = now + seaDays
```

Ice extras: `iceRisk` at the destination on arrival (+ escort fee and forecast text), `escortOut` fee
if leaving a port that is frozen. These, plus piracy, patrol, sanctions, inspection, pollution
worst case and voyage incident/sink risk, are shown before departure (rule 1, `ui/risk.js` etc.).

`depart`: if the ship is busy (loading, repairs) the plan is **queued** (`ship.plan`) and tender
commitments are made at once; `launch` runs when free: auto-refuel (buys up to need × 1.12),
canal tolls paid, escort-out paid, `voyage` object created, trip record, tender commit.

## Routing

- 0.5° sea/land grid; Dijkstra from the origin + string-pulling; distances in nm.
- Canals: `suez` (draft 20.1), `panama` (15.2), `nsr` Northern Sea Route (14, ice-dependent).
  Three Arctic corridors are open on the grid; the rest is pack ice.
- `DISTANCES[from][to]` = nm or `[nm, canals]`; `DISTANCES_CLOSED["nsr,panama"…]` holds only the
  pairs that change when those passages are closed.
- `ctx.closedCanals(state, draft, ship)`: Suez/Panama closed by event (`state.canals`), Panama
  draft limit during drought, NSR unless `nsrUsable` (open now and safe for `ICE.nsrHorizon` 20 days)
  and draft ≤ 14. The NSR is only routed when safe (never a gamble).
- After changing port coordinates or the map: `npm run build:world` / `build:distances`.

## One hour at sea (`sailHour`)

1. Waiting at a closed canal → idle burn until it reopens.
2. Stalled (breakdown, piracy, patrol) → idle burn.
3. Weather at position (`weatherAt`): speed × (1 − 0.08 per force above 6, min 0.45) at force ≥ 7;
   condition < 50 slows; mecanique skill speeds up.
4. Crew XP; new point → note (human) or `aiTrain`.
5. Canal crossing: NSR re-frozen → icebreaker escort fee; Suez/Panama closed → stop and wait.
6. Progress; fuel burn (+6 % per force above 6); wear (+storm wear at force ≥ 9).
7. Storm ≥ 9 first encounter: cargo damage chance `0.25 + (force−9)×0.12 + (100−cond)/300`.
8. Incident roll ([09](09-sea-risks.md)); severe with polluting cargo → partial spill; possible sinking.
9. Piracy roll; coastguard patrol roll (only with a parcel).
10. Sinking if condition 0, or < 22 in force ≥ 10 storm.
11. Out of fuel → emergency bunkering (30 % tank at 2.5× price + 40 k$, 24 h stop, trust −1).
12. Arrived → `arrive`.

## Arrival sequence (`arrive`)

1. Slow-steaming virtue (`slowVirtue`, only if speed ≤ 95 % of service, full at ≤ 75 %, scaled by
   sea days up to 10).
2. Port fee; first visit to a port → trust +0.5 (and "Toutes les escales" record progress).
3. Seasonal port already frozen → escort in, ship `iced`.
4. `checkSanctions` (taint in enforcing port), `contrabandOnArrival` (customs, parcel delivery),
   `darkOnArrival` (gang grudge, dark cargo, dark-job offer).
5. Seized by gang/justice → unloading waits.
6. Port-state inspection (detention → forced repair, fine, trust −3).
7. Bogus hold if a rival bought the port's officials (75 %).
8. Contract cargo → unloading → `deliverContract` (unless detained/held, which start after).
   Otherwise detention/hold starts, or family contract + "arrived" note.

## Seasonal ice (`sim/ice.js`, `ICE`)

Seasonal places: churchill, nome, sabetta, prudhoe_bay (port `ice{open, close, spread}`) and the
NSR (`ICE_PASSAGES.nsr`: open day 200, close 315, ±10).

- Each year's thaw/freeze date is random within the window (reproducible hash of seed/place/year).
  Everyone sees the window; the exact date is announced `ICE.announce` (14) days ahead
  (`iceForecast`, `forecastText`). Ice class adds `ICE.iceClass` (21) days at both ends.
- `iceRisk(state, id, tArr, ship)` = probability the place is frozen at arrival, from the known
  window — shown before departure.
- Escort fee `(60 000 + 2.5 × dwt) × (ice class ? 0.4 : 1)`, paid to enter a frozen port, to leave
  one, or when the NSR refreezes before the ship crosses.
- Contracts: no offers from a seasonal port unless ice-free for 4 days; destination seasonal only if
  deliverable before the freeze, or (end of season) reserved to ice-class ships at ×1.3 pay. On
  freeze, open offers from/to that port are removed.
- `iceDaily`: per-port news; one global freeze/thaw notification when 80 % (`ICE.season`) of seasonal
  ports changed; the 14-day announcement alerts only companies with a ship at, bound for, or planned
  for that port; ships caught by ice get a choice (wait laid up, or pay an escort) — rule 2.
- AI never gambles on ice (`tripCost` rejects `iceRisk > 0`); no used ships listed in ice ports.

## Standing orders (`sim/orders.js`, `ORDERS`; UI `ui/orders.js`)

Owner's design: automation is earned and paid for, receipts stay manual ("cashing in gives dopamine"). Two modes,
picked in the ship sheet's "Ordres" row under the status line (À la main · ↻ Ligne régulière · 🧭 Capitaine libre).
A locked chip says on click what is missing. `ship.orders` = `{kind: 'line' or 'captain', …, calling, since}`; `null`
= by hand.

| Mode | Gate | Cost | Taking control back |
|---|---|---|---|
| ↻ Regular line | crew confirmé or better and `crewMaxed` (all 6 XP points) | none | free (stop the line any time) |
| 🧭 Captain's choice | crew level 3, vieux briscards (`BRISCARD_LEVEL`) | `captainFee` 2 × a basic crew's daily wages, every day (book `equipage`, not while laid up) | free when the captain called you; otherwise `loseBriscardPoint` (−1 Vieux briscards rank, XP one step back, crew back to `crewWas`), stated in the confirmation with the days at sea to earn it back |

- **Line** (`setLine`, from the Set sail card: tick "En faire une ligne régulière", button "Lancer la ligne"; or the
  chip on a ship at sea / with a planned departure turns the current trip into a line). Two ends `a`/`b`, the speed,
  and the traded good if the cargo was bought at the departure port (`trade: {at, good, qty}`, bought again in cash
  each time). At each end (`lineStep`): sell the line cargo, re-depart contract cargo, repair at a yard of either end
  below `repairBelow` 60 % (to `repairTo` 92), then pick the best of: contracts to the other end (`pay − trip cost`)
  and the line trade at its buying end (sale − purchase − trip − empty return, cash only). Sails empty when a load
  waits at the other end; otherwise waits and calls after `lineWaitDays` 3. A negative line trade calls ("la marge
  passe sous zéro").
- **Captain** (`setCaptain`, dialog with limits, remembered in `ship.captainLimits`): never pirates / embargo /
  polluting cargo / oil (each can be allowed), longest voyage (`MAX_DAYS_CHOICES`), cash trade budget without loans
  (`TRADE_CHOICES`), repair threshold. `captainStep` scores options with the AI's own `jobOptions` and `bestOutlet`
  (see [16](16-ai.md)) with no estimation noise and no cargo loan, filtered by the limits, hostile ports, paid pirates
  and worn-ship-to-strict-port (detention). Sells traded cargo at the best outlet, repairs, goes to the nearest yard
  under 52 % with no yard here; when nothing fits, waits a day if this port has work, then repositions to the port
  with the most fitting contracts per distance (never back where it came from); calls after `callDays` 5.
- **Rule 1**: a line accepts the route flags shown when it is set (`routeFlags` both ways: pirate zone, paid pirates,
  embargo, gang grudge, bought authorities, single-hull check); a new flag, ice on arrival or a worn ship bound for a
  strict port makes it call (`newRisk`). The captain never takes a risk outside the limits. Seizures, hijacks, dark
  cargo: `ordersHour` does nothing, the usual decision windows apply.
- **Calling** (`o.calling` = the reason): the ship stops, logs "Appel du capitaine", and emits an `idle` note
  "Le X vous appelle : …" so time stops and its sheet opens. The orders box shows the reason with ▶ Relancer
  (`resumeOrders`), Reprendre la main (free) and, for the captain, the limits. A manual departure of a calling ship
  drops its orders (free). Laying up a ship under orders asks to take control back first.
- While on orders and idle in port, the sheet shows an "Ordres" tab instead of Contracts / Trade / Set sail; the
  status line reads "ligne régulière, il repart seul" or "le capitaine cherche un chargement"; the fleet list shows
  ↻ / 🧭 (📣 when calling). `onOrders` (orders and not calling) and `waitsForOrders` (idle in port and not on orders)
  replace `isIdle` wherever the UI counts ships waiting for the player (alerts, fleet, auto-resume, Tab).
- Hooks: `game.stepHour` runs `ordersHour` for every human ship with orders (after the AI loop's branch, so the AI
  RNG order is unchanged); `daily` runs `ordersDaily` per ship; `note()` drops `idle` notes of ships on orders.
