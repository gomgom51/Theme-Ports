# 16 · AI companies

Main file: `sim/ai.js` (`aiShipIdle`, `aiDaily`, `buyOne`, `efficiency`, `takeParcel`,
`avoidsEmbargo`, `embargoCost`). AI also lives in: `takeover.aiTakeover`, `plots.aiPlots`,
`dark.maybeDarkOffer` (accept/refuse), `clients.aiDenounces`, `crew.aiTrain`, `game.deliverContract`
(auto-collect, contest > 150 k withheld at 50 %), `piracy` (always pays ransom), `dark.seize`
(pays if cash > 2× ransom), `game.arrive` (hires a lawyer if cash > 3× fee), `home.homeDaily`
(shadow AI opts into the shadow lender).

AIs use the same actions and rules as players.

## Company setup

`ai = { skill (difficulty 0.7/1/1.3), nextFleet, daring, leaning, noEmbargoUntil, pool?, noTrade? }`.
`daring` = shadow leaning, or 50 % unless virtuous. Leanings (`AI_LEANINGS`) are shuffled per game
then aligned with home ports (shadow port → `ombre`, hub → `conquerant`).

| Leaning | Aims at | Specific behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| batisseur | legend (records) | fleet cap ×1.35 (`AI_BUILDER_FLEET`), prefers ship families it lacks (×3–4) |
| vertueux | reputation | avoids embargo, never attacks, values aid, denounces more, avoids oil (35 % of revenue penalty, no tankers bought) |
| conquerant | domination | double takeover chance, pays up to 2.9× worth, triple attack chance, targets cheapest prey |
| ombre | shadow fleet | ignores embargo stance (prices fines), seeks dark ports, accepts dark jobs 75 % (refuses if detection > 22 %), installs hidden compartments, opts into shadow lender, may buy embargoed companies |

## Ship idle loop (`aiShipIdle`, each hour a ship is idle)

Order of priorities:
0. Dark cargo on board → go deliver.
0b. Near the inspection threshold (+6) and a yard here → repair to 92 (borrow if needed).
0c. Contract cargo still on board → depart (borrow for fuel).
1. Traded cargo on board → sell here or carry to a better port (`quoteSellAt` at arrival, trip
   cost, embargo avoidance/cost, gang grudges).
2. Condition < 66 with a yard → repair to 92 (or sell an old hull whose repair costs > 2× value);
   condition < 52 without a full yard → sail to the nearest one.
3. Score all options per day: contracts here (`checkContract`, embargo avoidance/cost, hostile
   ports, grudges, paid pirates on route, deadline, price war, image factor, oil shame, aid value)
   and trades from here (cash or origin financing within `loanRoom`, ≥ 40 % hold fill, arrival price
   with tenders, handling, interest, `AI_CAPITAL_COST` 40 %/yr on locked cash, risk margin
   `vol × √days × 1.2`, embargo cost). Take the best positive one (and a matching parcel if daring).
3b. Prospect (25 %) once per call.
3c. Daring: take a ≥ 250 k parcel that repositions the ship.
3d. Shadow: sail to a dark port (40 %), preferring countries not yet done.
4. Reposition toward ports with fitting open contracts (35 % even if here has work); no ice.
5. Idle > 14 days and freight < 90 % → lay up. Otherwise wait 18–48 h.

`avoidsEmbargo` = not shadow and (enforcing flag, or virtuous, or fined within a year).
`embargoCost` = 85 % of the extra sanctions fine; refuses (∞) if the possible fine >
`EMBARGO_FINE_MAX` (30 %) of net worth.

## Daily (`aiDaily`)

- Treasury: borrow to cover negative cash; repay when cash > 4 M$ + 2 months of fixed costs.
- Crew level by ship price/age; armour (barbeles > 3 M$, citadel > 8 M$, hidden compartments for shadow).
- Reactivate laid-up ships when freight ≥ 97 % and cash > cost + 400 k; sell a ship laid up > 1 year
  while in debt; sell a ship itself when close to bank seizure.
- Fleet purchase every 30–60 days: skip if debt > 50 % fleet (unless no ship); fleet "points"
  `Σ max(0.4, √(dwt/50 000))` under `AI_FLEET (10) × (0.5 + 0.5 skill) × builder bonus`;
  prudent budget in weak markets or after a losing month. Rich (> 15 M$, freight ≥ 95 %, no order) →
  new build at Shanghai weighted by capacity/price and port reach. Else up to 3 used ships
  (`buyOne`: condition > 45, weight `dwt^0.65 / √(price + fix) × reach × efficiency³ × novelty`).

## Lessons (CLAUDE.md, keep in mind)

Every AI decision touching an embargoed port must check `avoidsEmbargo` and price `embargoCost`
(including selling cargo already on board). Laid-up ships must come back; ships laid up > 1 year are
sold while in debt. An AI with no ship may buy one despite debt. A shadow AI must refuse jobs over
22 % customs risk. The AI must charge itself the opportunity cost of cash locked in cargo.
